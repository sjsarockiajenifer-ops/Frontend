📖 How to Use & Update Your Index Page

1. Marking Tasks as Done:
Click the status button on any card or select "Completed". The progress bar updates instantly, and your completion status is safely saved in your browser.


2. Adding New Files / Exercises:
Whenever you create new HTML tasks in your folders, simply double-click update-tasks.bat in this directory! It will automatically scan and update the catalog while preserving your completed statuses.


3. Live In-Page Preview:
Click the "👁️ Preview" button on any card to test your webpage directly inside this dashboard in Desktop, Tablet, or Mobile views.


4. Notes & Personal Logs:
Click "📝 Note" on any task card to record notes, tricky points, or feedback from your mentors.

💾 Progress Backup & Sync

You can export your completed progress to a JSON file as a backup, or restore previously saved progress.

Need a fresh start? Resetting will clear all completed statuses from your browser storage.
